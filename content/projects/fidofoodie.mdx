## What it is

FidoFoodie is where the DogFoodie community gathers: finding datasets, validating them together, and building up a shared, searchable memory of what the community has learned.

## How it's built

Supabase vector and relational storage index the community's knowledge. Vercel handles dynamic community routing and edge rendering.
