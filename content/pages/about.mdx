I'm Darcy, a senior product designer who prototypes, builds, and documents with AI-native tooling.

This site is the record of that work. Every project has a working prototype, a walkthrough video, and an honest write-up of the trade-offs, because I think the clearest way to show design judgment is to explain it out loud.

## What I'm looking for

Remote senior product design roles where design and engineering work close together, and where being able to explain *why* a decision was made matters as much as making it.

## How I work

I move from discovery to a working prototype quickly, then build the real thing on Vercel and Supabase so the production concerns (auth, data, access policies) are part of the design from day one.
